Privacy Policy

ContentFlow — Last updated: May 20, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains what data ContentFlow collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Data We Collect

  • API credentials: Access tokens for YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram that you provide. These are stored locally on your device (macOS Keychain) and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Content metadata: Titles, topics, scripts, and performance metrics generated or fetched by the App, stored locally in a SQLite database on your device.
  • Usage data: The App does not collect analytics, crash reports, or telemetry.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate and publish content on your behalf to the platforms you connect.
  • To fetch performance analytics from those platforms and improve future content suggestions.
  • All processing happens locally on your device. No data is sent to ContentFlow servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the following third-party APIs on your behalf:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — for script generation. Your scripts are sent to Anthropic's API. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs — for voiceover generation. Scripts are sent to ElevenLabs. See ElevenLabs Privacy Policy.
  • Pexels — for stock footage. Search keywords are sent to Pexels. See Pexels Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube, TikTok, Instagram — for publishing and analytics. Their respective privacy policies apply.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal data. You can delete all data by removing the App's local database and credential files.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties beyond the API services required to operate the App (listed above).

6. Your Rights

Since all data is stored on your own device, you have full control at all times. You can delete your data by removing the App files and revoking any connected platform tokens from their respective dashboards.

7. Children's Privacy

ContentFlow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
